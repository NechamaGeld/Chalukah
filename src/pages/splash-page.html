Succos תשפ”ז-2026

We are pleased to once again offer an opportunity to order select food and paper goods for Succos, with two separate pricing tracks.

Programs

  • Shearis Yisroel Succos Fund
    This program is for mechanchim, klei kodesh, and individuals working in a Chicago mossad. You may choose subsidized or wholesale pricing, selectable at the start of the order.
  • Shearis Yisroel Co-op
    This co-op is for the general public, non-klei kodesh, and offers wholesale pricing for Yom Tov items.
A confirmation email from no-reply@bizmanagecrm.com will be sent after you place your order. Please check your spam folder.

If you do not receive a confirmation within one hour of placing your order, please email pesachfund@gmail.com.

Payment Details

  • Credit Card: Charged at the time of ordering.
  • Zelle: Must be sent within 24 hours to pesachfund@gmail.com.
  • Check — Full Payment: Payment should be dropped off at Katz, 5814 N St Louis Ave.
    Please place it in the mailbox. Do NOT ring the bell.
  • For any questions regarding payment, please TEXT Rabbi Katz at 773-406-4966.
  • Orders may be canceled if full payment is not received.

Important Points

  • You can print the list of available items from the top right of the ordering site.
  • Food items are for Yom Tov use. You may round up to a case.
  • Paper goods may be ordered for year-round use.
  • Orders are for your family only, not organizations or shuls.
  • Pick-up emails will be sent to the address provided. Pick-up appointments must be scheduled using the link in that email.

Fine Print

  • Prices are subject to change without notice.
  • We reserve the right to substitute items and edit quantities based on product availability.
  • No refunds or billing for errors under $10.
  • Check your order before leaving — we are not responsible for errors after pickup.
  • NO REFUNDS for items not picked up.

Contact

  • For questions or to volunteer, email pesachfund@gmail.com.

Distribution Information

  • Tentative pickup date is Tuesday, September 1st. (Self-serve only)
  • Look out for the email with updated pickup information.
